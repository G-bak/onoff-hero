# ON&OFF card showcase — generated artwork

2026-09-11. Built-in image generation. Each image is an original flat texture; the application supplies the actual 3D geometry. The reference screenshot and external video are study material only. No external screenshots or source shaders are shipped.

No perspective, no curvature, no device, no environment, no shadows outside the rectangle. It will be bent by code. Most of the image is a vivid orange (#F67B36) rectangular identity card inset with gently rounded top corners and small black typography. Large confident black "ON&OFF" at its top left. A small cropped photographic portrait at upper right of a fictional adult Korean woman creative director with a dark bob haircut, white tank top, neutral background. White text labels in a precise contemporary grotesk: "CREATIVE AGENCY", then prominent "One team.", "Many minds." Farther down small simple labels "Brand strategy", "Design & content", "Digital experiences". At lower right a prominent black and white QR-like abstract square mosaic on a white tile, decorative only. Large clean negative space. Orange card almost fills the black screen; no big empty footer. Immaculate editorial web UI, legible sparse typography. Treat this as one original ON&OFF concept, not a real client case study. Avoid phone chrome, rounded outer device frame, extra objects, gradients, bevels, watermarks. Image itself is the complete flat screen.

## Preparation

`node scripts/prepare-card-showcase.mjs` converts the original PNGs to WebP at width 780, quality 88, effort 6, without enlarging. It also derives normalized title glyphs from locally installed Montserrat Black 900 under the existing SIL Open Font License. Generated website assets are committed; original PNGs and prompts stay here for future edits.
